Source

This speech inspired me on the style choice of my poster design. I decided to do something attractive, vibrant, and tech with a center object, high contrast, and storytelling.

Design Process

Procreate-Inspiration Rough Sketch

This is my first version of my sketch. I wanted the colors to be highly saturated and in line with the characteristics of ITP at the same time, so I took the guide bar's purple of the event's official webpage as the background color. And I chose to use some forms of waves to represent the high-tech trait of ITP.

Photoshop-Refined Sketch

This is the second version of my sketch. I thought a spectrogram is ideal for the waveform. A spectrogram is a visual representation of the spectrum of frequencies of a signal as it varies with time. When applied to an audio signal, spectrograms are sometimes called sonographs, voiceprints, or voicegrams. I came across with this beautiful spectrogram when googling. Therefore, I assgined it to be the main object of the whole poster.

The pink is also from the event's official webpage.

I chose the Source Code as the font because it is a coding monospaced font reminding people of programming (one of ITP traits).

The source of the spectrogram

The left one is RGB and the right one is CMYK.

Illustrator-Refined Final Outcome

After settling down on the overall look, I imported the file into Illutrator to do the final spacing and griding.
