Refik Anadol, Unsupervised

The work takes the form of an AI-driven dynamic video installation that generates ever-changing visual imagery. It utilizes classic art collections—specifically the MoMA collection—as one of its “source material pools” to create new visual content. Anadol incorporates site-specific inputs from the Gund Hall environment—light, movement, acoustics, and external weather variations—to influence the evolving images and sounds.
