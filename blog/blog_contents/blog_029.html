What is interactivity to me?

For me, “interactivity” is more than just the superficial cycle of “player presses button, system responds.” It's a process of establishing a dynamic relationship between creator and experiencer. It means the work is no longer unidirectionally transmitted. Instead, it is reshaped by the audience's actions, choices, and even emotional responses. This “responsiveness” makes the experience alive, open-ended, and impossible to fully predetermine. At a deeper level, interactivity embodies a shared exchange of power and meaning—creators relinquish absolute control over the narrative, inviting audiences to become co-authors. In this process, technology serves merely as a medium; what truly matters is communication, empathy, and a sense of participation.

Does it add or detract to artmaking/storytelling - or when doesn't and when does it?

When interaction serves expression and resonance, it is art; When interaction becomes a distraction, it is an obstacle.

How did the interactivity in Gone Home contribute to or change the effect of the story?

Gone Home uses the player’s interaction with the environment to build atmosphere and gradually reveal the story. As the main character, you can wander through a huge, dimly lit house, exploring rooms and secret doors, picking up notes and letters scattered around. The gameplay carries a strong sense of curiosity and discovery.

The central narrative focuses on Sam’s love story, while side stories about her parents’ careers and emotional struggles add depth. Combined with fragmented atmospheric details—like rumors about the house being haunted or the séance setup—the storytelling feels rich and multilayered.

However, in my opinion, the game also has some flaws. There are too many repetitive interactions that provide no new information: countless drawers you can open only to find them empty (the developers could have simply made these drawers unopenable), and too many trivial objects like dishes, cleaning sprays, or mugs that can be picked up but offer nothing meaningful. These small design choices gradually wear down the player’s patience and interrupt the flow of the experience.
