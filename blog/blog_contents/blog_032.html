2025-11-04

We had an initial idea of making a praying machine in the form of a Guanyin shrine.

2025-11-05

We emailed Tom and got his suggestion: "I think the main thing missing from this is any form of sustained interaction. I could put a sacred offering on the plate or an old shoe on the plate, and either one would receive the same reward. Perhaps think about what you can do with the interaction that encourages the participant to continue paying attention, observing, reflecting, praying, or whatever behavior you are trying to inspire. Perhaps they have to remain in a pose that you are sensing? Continue a chant or a movement? Maintain the state of a sacred object, e.g. hold it upright when its resting position is horizontal? Continue a series of actions, like touching a number of holy stones in sequence, or at a given pace? All of these are examples of sustained interaction. Think about how you can make your project something we continue to work with, rather than activate, then passively watch, or worse, activate, then ignore."
