1.2-The Computer for the 21st Century

I have a feeling that this world is caring less and less about the real world and focusing more on the virtual world like VR, the exact opposite of the vision in the article. I am thinking about the importance and meaning of emphasizing the physical world in a context like this.

What's the boundary of being "ubiquitous"? Do we still need any screens?

The article mentions that the location of the ubiquitous computers matters. It reminds me of somebody saying that spatial intelligence is scarce in computer science and should be further developed.

The article paints a very promising picture of the future. However, what concerns me most is that the world's average economic level still doesn't allow for such a vision to materialize, and it's likely to remain that way for the foreseeable future. Based on my experience, a significant number of college students in China cannot afford laptops to use for study. The public equipment in schools is also quite outdated.

Unfortunately, access to electronic devices remains a privilege. I hope to see the day when everyone in the world can natrually regards computer as a part of their daily lives.
