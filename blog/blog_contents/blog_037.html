1-Dream

After immersed in different technology in the first semester in ITP, I decided to bring the focus of both my creative work and my career back to art. The dream is to build a lasting artistic practice by accumulating works and experience for the future.

2-Vision

This project will be a set of game concept art from 2D to 3D ideally.

3-Goal

3.1-Overall art Style: Horror and dark, reference: Richard Hugo

3.2-Technique: Digital hand-drawing, 3D modeling, perhaps material rendering

3.3-Design: World setting design, character design, environment design

3.4-Final outcome: One map, four character designs (one of which will be developed into a 3D model with stylized rendering if time permitted), one scene design/illustration.

3.5-Goal: Submit the project to competitions and upload it to ArtStation to help build my personal portfolio

4-Plan

Week 2: Research, world setting design, and gameplay design

Week 3-4: Map

Week 5: One scene design

Week 6-8: Four character designs

Week 9-13: Modeling and redering

Week 14: Typesetting
