2-One Peice of Data Visualization

3.01-What does the chart/graph/visualization represent?
3.02-Is there a clear title and purpose?
3.03-Is the title informative/accurate/misleading/vague/clickbaity/humorous/catchy?
3.04-Can you understand the intent of the visualization in a few moments?
3.05-Is there enough auxiliary information to help you decode the data?
3.06-What insight into the data does the visualization provide?
3.07-Do any particular patterns or trends emerge?
3.08-What new questions does it inspire?
3.09-Are there any outliers - data points that stand out as very different from the rest of the data - that should be reviewed or explored more closely?
3.10-Is this all of the data?
3.11-Does it look like the author deliberately excluded or hid relevant data?
3.12-Can you answer all of your questions with what you see, or do you need more data?
3.13-Is it clear what has been measured, and what the numbers represent?
3.14-Has the author included helpful notes, labels, and navigational hints?
3.15-Can useful and actionable data be gathered from this visualization?
3.16-Is the data multivariate? What is the level of granularity?
3.17-What is the data’s pedigree?
