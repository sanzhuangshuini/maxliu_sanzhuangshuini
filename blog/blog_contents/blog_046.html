1-Map

In this map, I attempted to mimic the company's confidential construction documents. To achieve this, I overlaid a layer of noise to simulate the texture of aged printed paper.

2-Scene Design

2.1-The "Red Vein" (Left)

This scene serves as the central concept for the entire project. Human bodies are fused into a crimson “microbial colony,” radiating outward from a core and burrowing into the mineral layers. During the drawing process, I discovered that mineral veins and microbial colonies share similar visual expressions. However, I don't believe I've fully captured this observation in this scene drawing. Perhaps I need more experimentation or a closer shot.

At the same time, I incorporated a rich array of hues with varied color tones into the background to convey the texture of minerals and create an eerie atmosphere.

2.2-The Walls in Cell Levels (Right)

To better establish the texture of the mineral vein, fungus, and organic matter, I focused on depicting the walls in Cell Levels.

Maybe I will return and refine the scene designs after months.

3-Character Design

3.1-Character Silhouette

Usually character concept art starts with experiment on silhouette, which is composed by a set of fundamental shapes in a certain rhythm. This decide the characteristic of the character. I plan to design a few alternatives for each the protagonist and the villains.

3.2-Protagonist

I would like the protagonist to be a female data scientist working for the government. She was framed and sent to the Whiteridge Federal Penitentiary after uncovering the conspiracy between the government and Murkoff. The reason why the protagonist is an Asian woman is that we lack Asian female game protagonists and what's more important is that fungus is related with connection and proliferation, which is a horror that resonates more deeply from a female perspective.

She needs to look a bit like a nerd and seem out of place here. At the same time, she doesn't quite fit the stereotype. She works out, so she's in good muscle and has decent stamina, but she has a slight stoop, which makes her presence seem more subdued. In prison, inmates' uniforms are bright orange for easy observation, but the protagonist covered herself in blood and grime to blend into the darkness in the riot.

3.3-Villain

The keywords are: sub-boss, jail inmate, fungus (the concept of fungus here does not literally refer to mushrooms, but rather to the fusion and proliferation of human bodies), body and mind fusion, experiment stitches.

She is the failed result of an attempt to fuse multiple human wills and bodies; only the two parts with heads retain human consciousness, while the consciousness of the other parts has dissipated. The two heads have contrasting personalities. The head with hair is more outgoing, goal-oriented, and aggressive. The hairless head is introverted and somewhat timid; it typically does not take the lead in actions but uses its “arms” to assist the hairy head. The two heads often argue due to their incompatible personalities. Part of their conversation takes place through direct communication via their partially merged consciousness, while the rest occurs through speaking. However, the spoken portion often comes across as disjointed, because the mental communication is happening at the same time. The hairy head is in prison for serial murder and suffers from a series of mental illnesses, which is why she actively attacks the protagonist. I was inspired by The Exorcist, The Human Centipede, and Silent Hill f.
