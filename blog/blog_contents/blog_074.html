2-Character & Environment

I am responsible for designing and creating our customized character—Bax("B" for Brian, "A" for Abby, "X" for Max, and the "S" sound for Siddhi).

I came up with a few design sketches.

After some discussion, we decided to use the version featuring a small white figure curled up on the green grass. I started to model they and build the environment in Unreal. A PCG grassland and a toon shader is incorporated. Although I have described the process simply in this summary blog post, I invested a great deal of time and effort in the transition from the initial sketch to the 3D environment.

4-Rigging

I used Mixamo's Auto-rigger and added blendshapes in Blender to control eye opening and closing, just in case animations for closing the eyes or blinking were needed.

5-Environment Refinement

During the shoot, we made a last-minute change to our plan: each team member was assigned a season—spring, summer, autumn, or winter—based on their birthday. everyone chose a song that made them feel most intensely "hold" within their assigned season, and Brian improvised a dance routine to the selected track. So, based on the latest adjustments, I added the changes of the four seasons to the scene.
