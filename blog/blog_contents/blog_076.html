1-VR

I switched the level from third-person to first-person. Adapting it for VR wasn't difficult; the real challenge lay in designing the movement mechanics. Initially, I kept the WASD control scheme, but I found it extremely dizzying (I nearly threw up!). I then wanted to use controllers, just like the demonstration level shown in class, but the controllers I had borrowed were broken! They wouldn't pair with the headset (I tried everything—the batteries were in, though perhaps they were dead?). Consequently, I had to switch to gesture recognition. However, the gesture recognition via PC Link didn't work well; while it could detect the presence of my hands, it failed to recognize the actual gestures, leaving me unable to move.

I hope to check out a working controller before the next class and use it to control movement.
