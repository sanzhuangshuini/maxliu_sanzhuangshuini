2-Level Design

Luke proposed a tactile interaction involving wind, which we all thought was a great idea. Building on his concept, I designed a new level—now, players must combine wind mechanics with building blocks to navigate the map in ways that go beyond simply moving straight ahead.

3-Narrative

To complement the new level design, I created a simple narrative: the PC player takes on the role of a toy that has just come to life, and must persuade their wner—the VR player—to help them leave the room and explore the wider world. When the PC player reaches the end, the VR player needs to help open the door to set them free.

4-Environment Art

4.1-Static

To add visual interest to the walls and ceiling, I incorporated bunting and used MCP to generate a playful, dinosaur-themed wallpaper. At the same time, I removed some floor-level decorations to achieve a better balance of visual information.

4.2-VFX

Theoretically, wind is invisible. However, in games, visual cues are the most intuitive way for players to grasp mechanics. That is why I designed this ribbon-like particle effect to represent wind currents, allowing players to immediately understand that these serve as transport paths.

4.3-Shader

I collaborated with MCP to create a beautiful toon shader.
