3.2-Vertical Slice

This is my thesis project. I had been quite uncertain about the scope of the work to submit next May, but after talking with my producer, I've decided to present a vertical slice consisting of three levels: the introductory animation, the first dream-world level, and the first real-world level set inside a RV (Recreational Vehicle).

3.3-Blotter Mechanics

Another aspect I'm quite uncertain about is the protagonist's drug-use mechanic. In the initial design, the protagonist, Persia, struggles with substance abuse, and I planned to integrate this into the mechanics—though I've been somewhat concerned about censorship. However, after researching other released projects and discussing the matter with a few people, I've decided to keep this design element. My current preliminary idea is to incorporate something related to blotter art culture; for instance, the player might need to use their mouse to tear a small tab off a sheet of blotter paper within the game.
