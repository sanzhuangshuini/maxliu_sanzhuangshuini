Once all the audio was connected, I used the SubPac to generate vibrations that sync with the low-frequency sounds.

2.2.2-Wind

I'm adding physical wind with a TreeBox. The box can only switch outlets on and off, so I set each fan's speed by hand and use the outlets to pick which fan blows:

Low fan, in front: a light breeze halfway up the lift hill.

High fan, in front: the plunge. It turns on a second early so it's at full speed for the drop, and cuts off when the scene jumps to the night road.

Medium fan, behind: the chase, while the seat reverses away from the girl.

For now I only have the plunge fan. The other two are built in Unreal but switched off until I get more fans. All fans turn off when the game starts or quits, and a loose cable won't crash the game.
