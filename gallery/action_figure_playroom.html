02IdeaGame Concept
Two players, two scales

Game
Concept

This is a two-player cooperative game set in a children's playroom. One player builds within a VR environment, while the other plays a platformer on PC.

VR

The VR player views the room from a first-person perspective, physically placing building blocks and interacting with doors and windows in the environment.

PC

Meanwhile, the PC player controls a small character from a third-person perspective, using the blocks as platforms and navigating through wind currents generated by wind blowing in through the windows to reach the objective.
