Digital Guanyin Shrine

This installation is a Guanyin shrine that is connected to ChatGPT. When users hold the wooden stick and pressing the force sensing resistor on it, the pressure data will be sent to ChatGPT to generate a unique Bagua pattern for each user along with its corresponding interpretation, and print it out, thereby simulating the process of people praying to Guanyin and receiving oracle.
