Background Setting

This concept design is based on the Hmong people of China. In this alternate world, Jiuli Tribe, as the representative of the Hmong, is one of the dozens of civilizations that survived the flooding of the world in the mid-19th century. They occupied Kunlun Island, which used to be the Kunlun Mountain Range, and renamed it as Jiuli Island. According to the lifestyle of their real-world counterpart, the Hmong, they are a people who specialize in the art of witchcraft named in Chinese as 蛊, and live in harmony with nature.

Political Structure

Jiuli Tribe has adopted a democratic representative system that coexists with divine authority. Chi is the agent of the divine power on earth and is responsible for all matters relating to witchcraft and rituals. Separate from this is JiYu Council, which manages all political and secular matters.

Sectoral Flags

From left to right: the main flag of Jiuli Tribe features a butterfly, representing their mythical Mother Butterfly; the flag of the department responsible for witchcraft features snakes and centipedes, which are often used as ingredients in witchcraft; the flag of Jiyu Council features the Jiyu Bird, which represents wisdom in their mythology; and the flag of the department in charge of war features the Panhu, a mythological creature modeled after a dog.

Architectural Silhouettes

Monster

Filterable bristle earthworms are monsters that live in symbiosis with Jiuli people. They mutate from ordinary earthworms that absorb the toxic miasma above the sea. The bristles on their bodies used to filter the toxic air are utilized by Jiuli people in their masks. By wearing this mask equipped with bristles, Jiuli people can breathe freely in the miasma zone.
